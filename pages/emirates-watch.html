ARG / CIE153 SAE Dubai

The Emirates Watch

A large scale ARG project built for CIE153, combining a full concept website, original 3D and VFX work, concept art, and supporting documents designed to make the fiction feel real. I handled rendering, gas simulations, and fluid sims for the Entity video, and composited and animated the Craft video using a custom jiggle physics addon built by Adil Mishelov. Concept art was developed by Jasmin Kitzberger, audio by Yar Zar Aung, 3D modeling by Adil Mishelov, and visual design, posters, and articles by Samantha Nicole Schultz.

Website: hertz-blip.github.io/emirates-watch
